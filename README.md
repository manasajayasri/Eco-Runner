# EcoRunner

Eco-Runner VR: A Race to Save the Planet is an immersive virtual reality game designed to entertain and educate users about environmental conservation. Players navigate through VR world—collecting eco-friendly items and avoiding hazards linked to pollution and environmental degradation. The game leverages physical activity, requiring players to bend, jump, and fetch items, enhancing both the immersion and interactivity of the experience. The main goals of Eco-Runner VR are to provide an engaging gaming experience that also raises awareness about sustainability issues. As players progress, they witness the direct impact of their actions through the Restoration Meter, which visually transforms the polluted environments into clean and vibrant ecosystems as they collect items. This visual feedback loop not only rewards players but also educates them on the importance of sustainable practices. The minimal viable product (MVP) focuses on one playable world with full VR controls for movement, an obstacle avoidance mechanic, a working Restoration Meter, and a basic scoring system. These elements are designed to meet the dual goals of entertainment and education, ensuring players feel both challenged and motivated to learn about environmental issues. Overall, Eco-Runner VR aims to combine fun gameplay with educational content, creating a compelling experience that encourages players to think about their environmental footprint while providing an enjoyable and physically engaging VR game.
